# 6. Trust / FAQ

**Built to show the gaps, not hide them.**

We're not going to promise that every song in every library can be transferred perfectly.

Some tracks won't exist on YouTube Music.

Some won't have a confident match.

Some may require your attention.

**You'll know which ones.**

That's the point.

### **Why use this when YouTube Music already has playlist transfer?**

If you only need a basic transfer, YouTube Music's built-in option may be enough.

This is for people who care about **understanding the migration before it happens, handling large libraries, and getting a transparent result afterward.**

### **Could it transfer the wrong version of a song?**

Any automated matching system can encounter ambiguous tracks.

Rather than assuming every similar result is correct, the system uses multiple track signals and can flag uncertain matches.

### **What happens to songs that can't be found?**

They're reported as unavailable or unmatched.

You won't be given a misleading picture of your library by quietly counting missing tracks as successful.

### **Can I transfer thousands of songs?**

The product is designed around large libraries.

Larger transfers will use paid capacity as the product scales.

### **Is this a subscription?**

No forced subscription.

Small migrations are free. Larger migrations are planned around one-time payments.

### **Will you store my Spotify data?**

The product is designed to minimize retention of personal music-library data.

You'll be shown what access is required before connecting your accounts.

### **What if everything doesn't transfer?**

That's expected for some libraries.

The goal isn't to pretend every track has a destination.

The goal is to confidently move what can be matched and clearly show you everything that couldn't.
